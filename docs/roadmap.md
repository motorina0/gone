## Environment art pipeline

Both public locations are generated consistently from editable environment JSON, external original raster materials, and five projection definitions. Their committed SVG sources, baked 2× WebP backgrounds, occlusion masks, and light overlays are replaceable by Blender-assisted or image-generated final art without changing gameplay coordinates.
