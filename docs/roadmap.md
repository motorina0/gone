# Roadmap

Next: improve gesture pinch handling and path smoothing, richer observation feedback, artist-authored backgrounds and depth masks, content-driven multi-mission selection, multiple elevations/interiors, abilities and inventory, sound, and performance profiling on physical mid-range phones. Combat, multiplayer, backend services, and final art remain outside this proof of concept.

## Environment art pipeline

Vatra Central Station is the first alternate location: a contemporary fictional railway district after rain, generated consistently from editable environment JSON and five projection definitions. Its committed SVG backgrounds, occlusion masks, and light overlays are replaceable by Blender-assisted or image-generated final art without changing gameplay coordinates.
