# Mobile controls

The canvas uses pointer events, `touch-action: none`, responsive FIT scaling, safe-area padding, 100dvh, and controls at least 44px square. A short stationary contact moves the operative, a double-tap runs, and movement over the threshold pans without issuing an order. Two pointers pinch to zoom. The compact portrait dock keeps all essential controls visible, labeled for assistive technology, and keyboard-focusable. Exploration pauses when the document is hidden.
